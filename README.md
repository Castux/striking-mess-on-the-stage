# Striking Mess on the Stage

*(Because Of The Jealousy Of A Cute Divine Girl)*

The muses of Comedy and Tragedy fight for the love of Dionysus. While a tragedy is being played somewhere in the world, Thalia messes up the minds of the actors and makes them play a comedy, and Melpomene does the same in comedies. You play one of the muses, and must turn all the actors of your rival into actors of your Art.

Made in 48 hours by a team of two for **Mini Ludum Dare #14** (November 2009): code by Agnès Haasser (Tût-tûûût) and Noé Falzon, music by Noé Falzon, graphics by Agnès Haasser.

It was made with [LÖVE](https://love2d.org/) 0.5 and won't run as is on current versions. It was imported from Launchpad (Bazaar) in 2026, with its full history.

The original builds (2008–2010) are in the [releases](../../releases).
